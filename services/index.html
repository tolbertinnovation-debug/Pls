Container reference

Which Container Do You Need?

The three dry-container sizes that cover most cargo moving through the Freeport. Not sure which fits your consignment? Tell us what you are shipping and we will advise.

  • 20ft Standard

    20GP
    Dimensions
    6.06 × 2.44 × 2.59 m
    Imperial
    20' × 8' × 8'6"
    Capacity
    ≈ 33 m³

    Best for: Dense cargo — tiles, machinery parts, tinned goods.

  • 40ft Standard

    40GP
    Dimensions
    12.19 × 2.44 × 2.59 m
    Imperial
    40' × 8' × 8'6"
    Capacity
    ≈ 67 m³

    Best for: General mixed cargo and palletised consignments.

  • 40ft High Cube

    40HC
    Dimensions
    12.19 × 2.44 × 2.90 m
    Imperial
    40' × 8' × 9'6"
    Capacity
    ≈ 76 m³

    Best for: Light, bulky cargo — furniture, packaging, textiles.

Nominal external dimensions to the ISO standard, which is what a carrier quotes against. Internal capacity and permitted payload vary by container build and shipping line — we confirm the exact figures for your booking before cargo is committed.

Common questions

Helpful answers before your shipment moves

A quick guide to our coverage, capabilities and quote process.

Which transport modes can Peak Logistics coordinate?

We coordinate cargo movement by air, sea, road and rail, selecting and connecting the modes that fit the shipment and route.

Can you handle customs documents and clearance together?

Yes. Our customs brokerage and documentation services cover import and export paperwork, duties and taxes, bills of lading, certificates of origin, packing lists and related compliance requirements.

Do you provide warehousing and last-mile delivery?

Yes. Our specialized logistics and supply-chain services include bonded warehousing, inventory coordination, distribution and last-mile delivery solutions.

Can services be combined for one shipment?

Yes. Peak Logistics can coordinate multiple stages—from freight and customs clearance through warehousing and final delivery—under one accountable team.

Where is your office located?

We are located Opposite Freeport of Monrovia, Behind CONEX Gas Station, Bushrod Island, Monrovia, Liberia.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote form and share your cargo type, origin, destination and preferred timeline. The team can then review the requirements and respond with the next steps.
